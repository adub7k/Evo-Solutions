# Off-page SEO plan — Evo Solutions, Albuquerque

The website can only do on-page SEO. For a local service business, **the Google
Business Profile and reviews outrank almost everything the site itself can do**
for "near me" and "in Albuquerque" searches. This is the work that happens off
the site, in rough priority order.

Nothing here involves buying links, spinning content, or posting to directory
networks. Those work for a few months and then cost you the domain.

---

## Tier 1 — Do these first (highest return, lowest cost)

### 1. Google Business Profile

This is the single highest-leverage asset. Ahead of the website, ahead of
everything.

- **Categories.** Primary: _Window Tinting Service_. Secondary: _Car Detailing
  Service_, _Auto Restoration Service_. The primary category is the biggest
  single ranking factor in the map pack — make sure it's the one you most want
  to rank for.
- **Confirm the rename fully propagated.** The profile went from MAD Detailing
  NM to Evo Solutions. Check the name, categories and description all reflect
  the film side now, not just detailing.
- **NAP consistency.** Name, address and phone must match the website character
  for character: `Evo Solutions`, `3500 Vista Alameda NE, Suite A, Albuquerque,
NM 87113`, `(505) 420-5747`. Any mismatch anywhere on the web dilutes it.
- **Services.** List every service individually with its own description, and
  point each at the matching page on the site.
- **Photos.** Post regularly — Google weights recency. The same photos you upload
  to ShopFlow can go straight to the profile. Aim for a few a week.
- **Google Posts.** A short post weekly (a finished car, a seasonal note about
  heat). Low effort, keeps the profile active.
- **Q&A.** Seed it yourself with the real questions from the site's FAQ and
  answer them. An empty Q&A is a wasted slot.

### 2. Review acquisition — the biggest lever you have

18 reviews at 5.0 is a good rating and a **low count**. Competitors with 150
mediocre reviews will outrank a perfect 18 in the map pack. Getting this to
50+ matters more than any link you could build.

- Ask at pickup, every time, while the customer is standing next to a car that
  looks better than when they dropped it off. That's the moment.
- Send the direct review link by text the same afternoon. ShopFlow can automate
  this — one message, no chasing.
- Never offer anything in exchange for a review. That's an FTC violation and
  Google filters them anyway.
- Reply to every review, good and bad. Replies are a ranking signal and they're
  read by the next customer far more carefully than the review itself.
- Ask reviewers to mention the service and the vehicle naturally ("ceramic tint
  on my Tacoma") — those words help you rank for them.

### 3. Local citations

Consistent NAP on the sites that matter. Do these by hand, once, correctly:

- Apple Maps (Apple Business Connect) — significant and often skipped
- Bing Places
- Yelp
- Facebook Business Page
- Nextdoor Business — genuinely strong for neighbourhood-level ABQ trade
- Better Business Bureau
- Yellow Pages / Angi / Thumbtack if you want the lead flow

Skip paid "submit to 500 directories" services entirely.

## Tier 2 — Local relevance and links

### 4. Automotive and industry listings

- Your film manufacturer's **authorised installer / dealer locator**. This is
  usually a strong, genuinely relevant backlink and it sends buying traffic.
  Once we confirm which brands you carry (see VERIFY.md), get listed.
- Detailing and PPF industry association directories, if you hold membership.

### 5. Albuquerque business and community

- Albuquerque Hispano Chamber of Commerce / Greater Albuquerque Chamber
- Local BIDs or business associations for the Vista Alameda / North I-25 area
- Sponsor something real and local: a car meet, a youth sports team, a school
  fundraiser. These produce a genuine link and actual local awareness.

### 6. Partnerships and referrals

The highest-quality local links come from businesses that share your customer
but don't compete with you:

- Independent used car dealers and small lots (recon work, plus a referral link)
- Body shops and paint shops (they need PPF and coating, you need chip repair)
- Performance and wheel shops
- Motorcycle and RV dealers
- Real estate agents and property managers — they're the route into the
  commercial and home tint side, which is your least competitive market

Trade links and referrals with them explicitly.

### 7. Car community

- ABQ and New Mexico car club Facebook groups, Cars & Coffee meets
- Enthusiast forums for the models you work on most — participate genuinely,
  don't drop links
- Show up at local meets with the shop's work. The Porsche, the AMG GLE and the
  Blackwing in your gallery are the kind of cars other enthusiasts notice

## Tier 3 — Slower, compounding

### 8. Content-driven links

The `/guides` section is built to earn links, not just rank. The New Mexico
tint law guide is the one most likely to get cited by local blogs, forums and
news — it's a genuinely useful reference. Keep it accurate and current, because
that's what makes people link to it.

### 9. Local PR

- Pitch a seasonal story to local outlets: how hot a parked car actually gets in
  an Albuquerque summer, what the sun does to a dashboard at 5,300 feet. Local
  news runs this every June.
- Any milestone worth a mention: expansion, new equipment, hiring.

### 10. Project features

Photograph and write up notable builds properly. A full-body PPF on a 911 is a
story other enthusiasts share. Post them to the gallery and to social with the
vehicle named.

---

## What NOT to do

- Buying links, guest-post networks, or "SEO packages" that promise volume
- Paid directory blasts and auto-submitters
- Any review that wasn't left voluntarily by a real customer
- Keyword-stuffing the Business Profile name (e.g. "Evo Solutions Window Tint
  Albuquerque Best Ceramic") — it's against Google's guidelines and gets the
  profile suspended
- Duplicate location listings for a single shop

## Measuring it

Check monthly:

- **Google Business Profile insights** — calls, direction requests, and searches
  that surfaced you. Direction requests are the closest proxy for real intent.
- **Search Console** — impressions and position for "window tint albuquerque",
  "ceramic coating albuquerque", "ppf albuquerque", "auto detailing albuquerque".
- **Map pack position** for those terms, checked from an Albuquerque location.
- **Review count and average**, month over month.
- **GA4** — organic sessions, and the `generate_lead` conversion split by source.

Realistic expectation: on-page changes show in Search Console within a few
weeks. Map pack movement from reviews and citations takes two to three months.
Anyone promising faster is selling something.
